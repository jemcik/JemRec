Fixes for Android 17. On Pixels since the September update, Android no longer tells apps whether Developer options are on, so setup got stuck there; the card now has They are on already. After a reboot on a Wi-Fi network not yet allowed for Wireless debugging, the recorder stayed down without saying why; JemRec now says so and offers Allow this Wi-Fi. On phones with 16 KB memory pages, JemRec no longer runs in compatibility mode.
