1.1: listen at your ear (discussion #83). Hold the phone to your ear while a recording plays and it moves to the earpiece and the screen goes dark, as in a call; lower it and it goes back to the loudspeaker. A button on the player shows where it plays and moves it. Settings → Playing → Play recordings: the loudspeaker, the loudspeaker with the earpiece at your ear, or the earpiece always. Headphones come first. Also: setup asks to keep JemRec paired past Android's 7-day limit.
