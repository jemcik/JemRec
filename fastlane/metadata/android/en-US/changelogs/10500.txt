1.5: Wireless debugging is on only while JemRec starts its recorder. It switches it on for the few seconds a start takes and off again once the recorder is up; USB debugging stays on, and one you switch on yourself stays on. A start that used to find it on takes about four seconds longer. On Android 12 and 13 a recorder that stopped by itself no longer waits for a tap on the header. Turning on Hide the debugging notification or Show who called starts the recorder first if it is down.
