1.7: With a PIN, pattern or password and a data mode such as File transfer as Developer options' Default USB configuration, every lock and unlock could stop JemRec's recorder (#131). JemRec now says so on the home screen and in a notification, and Set to No data transfer fixes it with one tap; a computer then only charges the phone until you choose File transfer in its USB notification. Also: one unlucky lock after a start could leave Wireless debugging on for good; now it takes two in a row.
