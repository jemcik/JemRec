1.9.1: on Android 12 and 13, JemRec could close by itself when Android paused it while it worked out a waveform for the player or prepared a recording to share: Android took the decoder away and freed its memory under JemRec. Now JemRec touches that memory only to copy in or out, which leaves a chance of microseconds, and a decoder taken away means a waveform worked out at its next turn, or Share tapped again.
