A Calls tab shows the phone's call log: a recorded call plays from it, and one that was not recorded says why, where JemRec knows. Settings can narrow down whose calls are kept - only some contacts, all but some, or none, and calls with numbers not in your contacts - and can name files with the caller in them. On an Honor, files can be named the way Honor's own Phone app looks for them, so its call history shows a play button.
