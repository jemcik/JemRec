Fixes from a review of the whole app. Kept calls are no longer lost when call waiting is answered after the first call ends, after a quick redial, or on a failed contacts lookup. A recording that cannot be written is kept for a retry, never lost or saved twice. Caller-ID names for numbers not in contacts are back (#30). Auto-delete leaves other apps' audio alone. The recorder survives more errors, and setup recovers from more failures. Messenger calls are offered where Android allows them.
