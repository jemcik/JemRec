An answering machine for phone calls, off until you switch it on. It answers the calls you don't take, after a delay you choose, plays your greeting with your microphone muted, and saves the caller's message, with a notification. You can still take the call. It follows Whose calls to keep, and never answers during another call or a messenger call, while roaming, or for a day after you call an emergency number. Built-in checks and a test call tell whether it works on your phone. Experimental.
