Three looks, in Settings → Theme: Memo, the app as it was; Deck, a pocket recorder with an LCD and a REC key; Multitrack, a sound editor, each caller in a colour of their own - each light, dark or as the phone. A recording plays as its waveform: tap or drag to go there. Both tabs can be searched and filtered with chips, and a call's row looks like a recording's. Settings is drawn in each look, its rows wearing the header's marks.
