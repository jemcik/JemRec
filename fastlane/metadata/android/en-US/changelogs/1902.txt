Recordings look the same after you reinstall JemRec. Which are messages, which you kept and who each messenger call was with are now written inside each recording, so a reinstall reads them back: messages stay messages, and a messenger call keeps its caller instead of reading "Telegram call". This holds for recordings made from this version on. On Android 12, JemRec now asks to read photos and media to list recordings from before a reinstall, as it asks for audio on 13 and later.
