Wi-Fi Calling. On a Galaxy S23, where a Wi-Fi call does not go through the phone's call path, calls were saved silent: JemRec now records both sides, and the answering machine answers them with its greeting heard. On a Pixel, an outgoing Wi-Fi call that rang first was thrown away as having no audio: each recording's sound is now placed by when it was captured, so the cut finds the call, and a recording of one call that the cut would leave empty is saved whole, not deleted.
