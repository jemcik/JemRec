Unplugging the phone from a computer no longer leaves it unrecorded. On an Honor it stopped Android's debugging service three seconds later, and the recorder with it, while both debugging switches still read on; nothing brought it back, and the home screen asked to allow a Wi-Fi already allowed. The recorder now watches the cable, and JemRec starts it again by itself, about 15 seconds after the unplug on Wi-Fi - with the screen off, when JemRec may start automatically. A charger never did this.
