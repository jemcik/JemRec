Start without Wi-Fi, in Settings, off by default: JemRec opens debugging port 5555, so the recorder can start with no Wi-Fi until the phone restarts, after Turn all debugging off too. Whenever USB debugging is on, the port is open to every network the phone joins, and announced there by the phone's serial number. A long press on the Quick Settings tile opens JemRec.
