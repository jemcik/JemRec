Fixed: after a restart, some phones (the Honor on MagicOS 11) left Wireless debugging switched on but not running, and JemRec stayed off until its header was tapped - while saying the Wi-Fi was not allowed. JemRec now sees that and switches Wireless debugging off and on itself: recording again seconds after the phone is unlocked. "Tap to allow this Wi-Fi" shows only when that is the cause, and the tap now starts the recorder too.
