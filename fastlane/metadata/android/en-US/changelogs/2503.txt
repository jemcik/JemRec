Fixed: since 0.5, JemRec's quick restarts of the recorder never ran - after a reboot, back on Wi-Fi, when the phone switched USB debugging off. Shrinking the release build removed a part WorkManager needs, so each failed before it started, and the recorder came back only at the 15-minute check or when JemRec was opened: a call in that gap was not recorded. They run again (issue #69), and back on Wi-Fi no longer waits behind an earlier try. WorkManager is now 2.12.0.
