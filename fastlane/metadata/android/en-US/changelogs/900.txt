JemRec now tells you what happened to each call without being opened. After each call, a notification shows who it was with and how long it ran, with Play, Share and Delete; Settings can turn it off. Others say when a recording came out silent, when the recorder is down and why (it needs Wi-Fi, or the pairing needs redoing), and when a call went unrecorded. Also fixes the recorder waiting for its next scheduled check, instead of seconds, to come back when Wi-Fi returned.
